Eva Publisher – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Eva Publisher is an internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels "Eva the Little Lion" and "Eva's Lion Family". It is used only by the channel owner to manage those channels.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to read and update metadata of videos on the owner's own channels: titles, descriptions, translations, audience (made for kids) settings, publishing schedules, playlists and thumbnails.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and publishing records are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server and does not collect, sell or share any personal data with third parties.

Google API Services

Eva Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
